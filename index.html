Questions

Before you ask

The questions almost every client asks before starting. Something else on your mind? Add it to your project brief and we'll answer it with your quote.

Can NovaStack build everything together: website, app and AI agent?+

Yes, that's exactly what we do. One team builds your complete stack, so your website, app, AI calling agent and automations share the same data and work as one system.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on the scope: a business website, a full web app and an AI calling setup are very different builds. Send us a brief using the builder above and we'll come back with a clear quote based on your exact requirements, with no hidden charges.

What does the AI calling agent actually do?+

It makes and answers phone calls in a natural voice: greeting customers, answering common questions, qualifying leads, booking appointments and following up. Every call is recorded and transcribed, and a human can take over whenever needed.

I already have a website. Can you add automation or an online store?+

Absolutely. We regularly extend existing setups, adding a Shopify store, a WordPress revamp, n8n automations or an AI agent on top of what you already have.

Do you provide support after the project launches?+

Yes. We hand over full access and documentation at launch, and stay available for updates, fixes and new features as your business grows.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what information NovaStack collects through this website and during client projects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can and we never sell personal information.

1. Who we are

NovaStack is a digital product agency. We design and build websites & web apps, mobile apps, AI calling agents, n8n automations, Shopify stores, WordPress sites, Meta ad campaigns, technical SEO and custom full-stack products for businesses. This policy covers this website and the enquiries and projects that come through it.

2. What the Project Brief Builder collects

While you are filling in the Project Brief Builder, it runs in your browser and nothing is sent to us while you type. When you click Send my brief, the services you selected, your name, your business or company name, your preferred timeline and your project details are sent to NovaStack through Formspree, the form delivery service we use for this form, and we receive exactly what you included.

You can also send the same brief yourself instead: the WhatsApp and email options alongside the form attach what you picked and typed to the message, so you can send it from your own WhatsApp or email. If you send a brief that way, we also receive the contact details of the channel you sent it from.

3. Information during a project

If you become a client, we may also handle information you give us to do the work, such as:

  • Content for your website, app or store, text, images, product details and prices.
  • Account access you share with us for tools we set up or manage for you, such as hosting, Shopify, WordPress or automation accounts.
  • Business information needed for AI calling agents and automations, such as call scripts, FAQs, lead lists and CRM data you provide.

We only ask for what a project actually needs, and we use it only to deliver that project and support it afterwards.

4. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote for the work you described.
  • To plan, build, test and launch your project.
  • To provide support, updates and fixes after launch.
  • To keep basic records of our work together, such as agreed scope, invoices and handover notes.

We do not use enquiry or client information for unrelated marketing, and we never sell it to anyone.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website is a simple, static site. It does not run advertising trackers and does not set marketing cookies. Your browser may store small technical items needed for the page to work and to remember your system’s light or dark appearance, the site simply follows your device setting and does not build a profile of you. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future to understand which pages are useful, we will update this policy first.

Websites, stores and apps we build for clients may use their own cookies and analytics (for example Shopify or Google Analytics), those are governed by the client’s own privacy policy, not this one.

6. Who we share information with

When you send a brief using Send my brief, that brief is delivered to us through Formspree, our form delivery service. We share other information only when it is needed to deliver your project, for example with hosting providers, Shopify, WordPress, payment gateways, telephony providers for AI calling agents, or automation tools such as n8n, and only with the services involved in your build. Wherever possible, these accounts are set up in your name and under your control. We never sell personal information, and we do not hand it to third parties for their own marketing.

7. How long we keep it, and security

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond and, if a project goes ahead, for the life of that project and a reasonable period afterwards for support and records. Project files and access are handed over to you at launch. We take reasonable care to protect information in our hands, using strong passwords and access controls on the tools we use, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please only share sensitive credentials through secure channels we agree with you.

8. Your rights

Your information belongs to you. You can ask us at any time to tell you what information of yours we hold, to correct it, or to delete it. If you ask us to delete it, we will, except for basic records we are required to keep for accounting or legal reasons. To make a request, email us at contact@novastackltd.com, call or WhatsApp us on +92 339 3887766, or reply on the same channel you originally contacted us on.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be easy to spot, we will not quietly expand how your information is used.

10. Contact us

For anything about this policy or your information, email us at contact@novastackltd.com or call / WhatsApp us on +92 339 3887766, or use the Project Brief Builder on this website, or reply on the channel you first contacted us through. We read and respond to every message ourselves.
